# Check 29: Maryland: check the Maryland rules if you call Maryland numbers

Group E: State laws, recording and vendors

## What to verify

If any person on your list is in Maryland, you follow Maryland's rules on written consent, hours, repeat calls and caller ID, and you know whether a Maryland exemption applies to you.

## Why it matters

Maryland Commercial Law 14-4502 bars a telephone solicitation that involves an automated system or a recorded message without the called party's prior express written consent. It bars calls between 8 p.m. and 8 a.m. in the called party's time zone, more than three calls to the same person in 24 hours on the same subject, and altering the caller's voice to disguise or deceive. It lists exemptions (for example single solicitations in response to an inquiry, and business to business sales by a caller operating for at least three years under the same name). 14-4501 defines prior express written consent, including that it be a signed written agreement. The kit did not read Maryland's penalty or enforcement sections.
