# Check 03: If you buy a list, read the terms and ask for proof

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

This is one of the 3 checks spoken in the Reel.

## What to verify

For every list you buy or rent, you have read the provider's terms, and the provider has given you written proof of consent for the people on YOUR list: who agreed, to what wording, on which page, on which date, and that the wording covers your business and AI voice calls.

## Why it matters

A provider saying 'our leads are opted in' is a claim, not proof. In January 2025 a federal appeals court threw out an FCC rule that said a person's consent could cover only one seller at a time. That ruling removed one extra restriction. It did not remove the basic need for prior express consent, and for marketing calls with an artificial voice the rule still asks for a signed written agreement that clearly authorizes the seller.

## How to fix

1. Ask the provider in writing for proof of consent for every record you bought: name, number, date, the page or form, the exact wording, and whether your business is named or clearly covered.
2. Read the provider's terms for where the leads came from, whether consent is shared with other buyers, and who is responsible if consent is missing.
3. Call only the records for which you hold proof naming you or clearly covering you. Keep the rest on the DO NOT CALL pile.
4. If a provider will not give proof, do not buy from them again.
5. Whether consent given to one lead provider can cover several sellers is a legal question that has moved. Ask a lawyer before you rely on it.
