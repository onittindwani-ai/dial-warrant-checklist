# Check 14: Say who is calling, that it is a sales call, and give a number that works

Group B: The AI voice and the first seconds of the call

## What to verify

At the start of the call the AI says your business name (the registered name), says what the call is about (selling something, if it is), and gives a number people can call back to ask not to be called, which is answered during regular business hours.

## Why it matters

For artificial voice messages the FCC rule requires the message to state, at the start, the identity of the business responsible for the call, using the registered name, and to give a telephone number that is not a 900 number and that works for do not call requests during regular business hours. The FTC rule requires telemarketers to say promptly and clearly who is selling, that the purpose is to sell, and what the goods or services are.

## How to fix

1. Rewrite the first 10 seconds of your script: your registered business name, that you are calling to sell or offer [what], and your callback number.
2. Test that the callback number rings through to someone, or to a service that records do not call requests, during business hours.
3. Use the same business name that is registered with your state.
