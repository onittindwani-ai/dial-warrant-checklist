# Check 23: Call only in the allowed hours at the other person's place

Group D: Calling hours, caller ID and your phone provider

## What to verify

Your AI dialer is set to stop at 8 pm (not 9 pm) in the called person's local time, and never starts before 8 am there. It uses the person's time zone, not yours.

## Why it matters

Federal rules bar telephone solicitations to a home before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. at the called person's location. Florida, Oklahoma and Maryland are tighter: Florida and Oklahoma bar calls before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m., and each also limits repeat calls (three in 24 hours on the same subject). Maryland bars calls between 8 p.m. and 8 a.m. in the called party's time zone and also three times in 24 hours.

## How to fix

1. Set one quiet window for all calls and texts: 8 am to 8 pm in the person's own time zone. It satisfies every source above.
2. Find the time zone from the person's address or ZIP code if you have it. An area code shows where a number started, not where the person is now.
3. Cap repeat attempts at three in any 24 hours on the same subject.
