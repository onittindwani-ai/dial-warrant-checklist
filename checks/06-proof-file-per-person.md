# Check 06: Save a proof file for every person you contact

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

For each person you call or text for marketing, you can pull up a record with their name, number, the date and time of their yes, the exact wording and page they saw, and where it came from.

## Why it matters

If someone complains, the proof is your defence. The FTC rule lists what a complete consent record holds: the person's name and number, a copy of the request as it was shown, the purpose, a copy of the consent and the date it was given. Vendors can also ask for it. Twilio's messaging policy, for example, says you must provide proof of consent, with the date and the method, when they ask.

## How to fix

1. Use templates/consent-proof-log.csv or your CRM (a system that stores your contacts). One row per person.
2. Store a dated copy of every version of the consent wording and form page.
3. Back the file up somewhere safe, and keep it away from public places.
4. Spot check: pick 5 people at random and see how long it takes to find their proof. If it takes more than a few minutes, fix the system.
