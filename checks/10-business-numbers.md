# Check 10: Do not assume business numbers are exempt

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

You have decided, with evidence, which of your numbers are cell phones, home lines and office lines. You do not treat a number as exempt only because it belongs to a business.

## Why it matters

The FTC's telemarketing rule exempts most calls between a telemarketer and a business, but with exceptions, such as calls selling nondurable office or cleaning supplies. The FCC's artificial voice rule for non-emergency calls covers any number on a cell phone service, whether the owner uses it for business or not. State laws add their own business-to-business exemptions with conditions (Maryland's, for example, asks that the caller has operated for at least three years under the same name).

## How to fix

1. Tag each number in your list as cell, home line, office line or unknown.
2. Treat every cell number and every unknown number as a consumer number for AI voice calls.
3. Ask a lawyer before you rely on any business exemption, and check the state of the person you call.
