# Check 20: Write a short do not call policy and train the people who use the tools

Group C: Do not call lists and stop requests

## What to verify

A written do not call policy exists (one page is fine), and everyone who uses your lists or AI calling tool has been told how it works and has signed or acknowledged it.

## Why it matters

The FCC rule says a written policy for the internal list must be available on demand, and personnel must be trained on the list's existence and use. The FTC safe harbor for honest mistakes also lists written procedures, training and monitoring.

## How to fix

1. Write one page: who owns the list, how stops are added, how often the registry is scrubbed, how long records are kept.
2. Hold a 15 minute walk through for everyone who uses the lists or the AI tool, and keep a dated sign off.
3. Check once a month that the AI tool is still applying your stop list.
