# Check 11: Treat every AI voice as an artificial voice

Group B: The AI voice and the first seconds of the call

## What to verify

You have written down every kind of call your AI makes (marketing, appointment reminders, follow ups) and decided for each one: is it marketing, who is called (cell, home line), and what consent you hold.

## Why it matters

In February 2024 the FCC confirmed that the law's rules on an 'artificial or prerecorded voice' cover current AI technologies that resemble human voices or generate call content. It said there is no carve out for technology that purports to be the equivalent of a live agent. Callers need the called person's prior express consent, and prior express written consent when the call introduces an ad or is telemarketing. Non-marketing calls to cell phones also need consent unless an exemption applies.

## How to fix

1. List your AI call types in a table: purpose, who is called, consent held.
2. Mark each type as marketing or informational. Marketing needs written consent (checks 01 to 05).
3. If a call is informational only, still hold consent for cell numbers, or confirm an exemption with a lawyer.
4. Do not describe your AI agent as 'a live agent' in a way that hides that it is AI.
