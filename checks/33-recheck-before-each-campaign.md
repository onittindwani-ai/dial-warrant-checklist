# Check 33: Recheck the rules before every campaign

Group F: Money and upkeep

## What to verify

Before each campaign you have reopened the sources of this kit, written the date, and noted anything that changed since the Date read in each check.

## Why it matters

Several rules moved recently. In January 2025 a court threw out the FCC's one-to-one consent rule. In January 2026 the FCC extended a waiver of part of its stop request rule to January 31, 2027. The FCC proposed AI call disclosure rules in 2024, and this kit could not confirm whether they became final. An amendment to the FCC rule published October 26, 2026 (91 FR 67219) was linked from the rule page and this kit could not open it. An AI assistant that was trained earlier will not know these changes.

## How to fix

1. Open each Source URL in docs/03-SOURCES.md for the checks you rely on, and write today's date.
2. Search for news on 'FCC TCPA' and your states' laws for changes since the Date read, and note them.
3. Ask a lawyer once a year to review your consent wording, your script and your policy.
