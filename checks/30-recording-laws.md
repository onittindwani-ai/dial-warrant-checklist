# Check 30: Tell people if the call or the AI is recording, and learn the recording law for your states

Group E: State laws, recording and vendors

## What to verify

If your AI platform records or transcribes calls, the AI says so at the start, you know the recording law of the states where your leads live, and you store recordings safely.

## Why it matters

Federal law allows a person who is a party to a call, or has one party's consent, to record it, unless done for a criminal or tortious purpose. Some states ask for the consent of everyone on the call. California's Penal Code 632, for example, makes it an offense to record a confidential communication without the consent of all parties (the kit read it on a mirror site; verify it on the official California site). AI calling tools often record and transcribe by default. This kit does not list states from memory, because the rules change.

## How to fix

1. Check your AI platform settings: is recording on by default?
2. Add 'This call may be recorded' to the opening line (sample wording), so consent is clear whatever the state.
3. Ask a lawyer which of your lead states need all party consent, and write them into your policy.
4. Store recordings only where you can delete them on request, and keep them private.
