# Check 28: Oklahoma: check the Oklahoma rules if you call Oklahoma numbers

Group E: State laws, recording and vendors

## What to verify

If any person on your list is in Oklahoma, you follow Oklahoma's rules on written consent, hours (8 am to 8 pm), repeat calls (three in 24 hours) and caller ID.

## Why it matters

Oklahoma's Telephone Solicitation Act of 2022 (HB 3168, effective November 1, 2022) bars a commercial sales call that uses an automated system or a recorded message without prior express written consent, bars calls before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m. local time and more than three in 24 hours on the same subject, requires caller ID transmission, bars a voice altered to deceive, and lets a called party recover actual damages or $500, whichever is greater, and up to three times that for willful or knowing violations. This kit read the enrolled bill, which says its new sections are to be codified as sections 775C.1 to 775C.6 of Title 15. Check the current numbered statute.
