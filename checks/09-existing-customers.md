# Check 09: Do not assume an existing customer said yes to AI calls

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

You do not treat 'they bought from us before' as written consent for marketing calls with an AI voice. Past customers get the same consent check as everyone else.

## Why it matters

For a marketing call with an artificial voice to a home phone, the rule asks for prior express written consent, and the exemptions it lists are things like emergency, certain non-commercial, informational, nonprofit and health care calls. A past purchase is not on that list. The rule does define an 'established business relationship' (a purchase in the last 18 months, or an inquiry in the last three months), but that definition belongs to the do not call rules, and it ends when the person says to stop. Florida's law treats calls to people with an existing business relationship differently, but the federal rule still applies.

## How to fix

1. Do not copy your customer list straight into the AI dialer. Check each customer for a documented written yes.
2. Where there is none, ask them to opt in with the consent wording from check 04 (an email asking them to tick a box is fine), then call only those who did.
3. Let a lawyer tell you whether a specific informational call to customers (for example a delivery update) is exempt for your case.
