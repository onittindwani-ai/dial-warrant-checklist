# Check 19: Keep your own do not call list

Group C: Do not call lists and stop requests

## What to verify

You keep a list of people who asked you not to call or text them, and every campaign is checked against it first.

## Why it matters

The FCC rule requires callers who make telemarketing calls to keep an internal (your own) do not call list, record each request and honor it within a reasonable time not more than ten business days. The FTC rule bars calling people who told the seller they do not want calls. Records of requests are kept five years.

## How to fix

1. Use templates/stop-request-log.csv or your CRM. One row per request: who, number, date, how they asked.
2. Add every opt out (key press, reply, email, voicemail, website) the same day.
3. Before any campaign, remove everyone on this list from the calling list.
