# Check 05: Make sure consent is signed, lists the number, and is never a condition of buying

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

Your consent is a written agreement with the person's signature (an electronic one is fine if valid), it lists the phone number, and it tells the person they do not have to agree in order to buy anything. Nothing in your checkout, booking or download flow is blocked unless they tick it.

## Why it matters

The federal definition of prior express written consent requires a signature, the number, and a clear disclosure that agreeing is not required as a condition of purchase. Consent that was forced to get something else is a weak yes.

## How to fix

1. Test your form with the box left empty. The person must still be able to download the item or book the call.
2. Keep the sentence 'I do not have to agree in order to buy anything' (or an equally clear one) next to the box.
3. Check the form stores the number the person typed and the time they ticked the box.
