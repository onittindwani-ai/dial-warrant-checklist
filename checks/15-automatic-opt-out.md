# Check 15: Offer an automatic way to opt out in the first seconds

Group B: The AI voice and the first seconds of the call

## What to verify

For marketing calls with an AI voice, the call offers an automated opt out (press a key or say a word) within two seconds of saying who is calling. Voicemails give a toll-free number that reaches the same opt out.

## Why it matters

The FCC rule requires an automated, interactive opt out mechanism within two seconds of the identification information for telemarketing artificial voice messages, and a toll-free callback number on messages left on answering machines. The FTC rule adds that the opt out must automatically add the number to the seller's own do not call list and then end the call.

## How to fix

1. Add a line right after your identification such as 'To stop these calls, press 9 now or say stop.' (sample wording).
2. Test the opt out yourself on a test phone and see that the number lands on your own do not call list the same day (check 19).
3. For voicemails, leave a toll-free number that reaches the opt out.
