# Check 12: Do not clone or disguise a voice to mislead

Group B: The AI voice and the first seconds of the call

## What to verify

Your AI agent does not use a cloned voice of a real person without that person's written permission, and no voice change is used to hide who is calling.

## Why it matters

The FCC said voice cloning technology falls within the artificial voice rules because a person is not speaking, and it warned a cloned voice can convince people a trusted person wants something. Florida, Oklahoma and Maryland each make it unlawful to intentionally alter the caller's voice to disguise identity in order to defraud, confuse or injure the person called, or to obtain personal information that may be used fraudulently.

## How to fix

1. Pick a voice that is plainly your assistant's voice. If you want your own voice cloned, you give your own written permission.
2. Never clone the voice of another person (an employee, a celebrity, the prospect's relative) without their written permission.
3. Never use a voice change to hide the business behind the call.
