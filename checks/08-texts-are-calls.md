# Check 08: Treat texts like calls

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

Every check in this kit that applies to calls also applies to the text messages you send to the same people: consent first, proof, stop requests, quiet hours, state laws.

## Why it matters

The FCC reads the word 'call' in the law to include text messages, as an appeals court noted in 2025. The FCC's stop rule names text replies (stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, cancel, unsubscribe). Florida's law lists text messages as sales calls and adds a rule that a person must first reply STOP before suing over texts.

## How to fix

1. Add your text list to the lead source register and the consent proof log.
2. Make the first text say who you are and how to stop ('Reply STOP to opt out').
3. Make sure your texting tool processes the stop words listed in the source above and also understands plain replies like 'please stop texting me'.
