# Check 26: Ask your provider to show it is in the Robocall Mitigation Database

Group D: Calling hours, caller ID and your phone provider

## What to verify

You have asked your phone provider to confirm, in writing, that it has filed with the FCC's Robocall Mitigation Database and is not removed from it, and you have saved the answer.

## Why it matters

Providers must file certifications in the Robocall Mitigation Database about their robocall prevention work. Other providers may accept calls directly from a provider only if its filing appears in the database. If your provider is not listed, other carriers can refuse its traffic, and your calls fail. A business cannot file this itself; it can ask.

## How to fix

1. Email your provider: 'Are you listed in the FCC Robocall Mitigation Database? Please send the name under which you are listed.' Save the reply.
2. If the provider cannot answer, choose another provider.
3. Ask again each March (providers recertify yearly).
