# Check 04: Make the consent wording cover AI voice calls and name your business

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

The wording next to your consent box says, in plain words: who may contact the person (your business name), by what means (calls and texts), that the calls may use an automated system or an artificial or AI-generated voice, and which number they are giving.

## Why it matters

The federal definition says the agreement must clearly authorize the seller to send ads or telemarketing using an autodialer or an artificial or prerecorded voice, and must list the number. The FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI voices that sound human, including cloned voices, count as artificial voices. Wording that only says 'you may hear from us' leaves a gap.

## How to fix

1. Use clear wording such as the sample below, with your own business name. It is a sample, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it once.
2. SAMPLE WORDING: By ticking this box and entering my number, I agree that [Your Business Name] may call or text me at this number with marketing messages, including calls that use an automated system or an artificial or AI-generated voice. I do not have to agree in order to buy anything.
3. Show the wording on the page itself, not behind a link only.
4. Save a dated copy of the wording each time you change it (check 06).
