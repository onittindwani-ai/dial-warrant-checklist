# Check 17: Get the National Do Not Call Registry for your area codes

Group C: Do not call lists and stop requests

## What to verify

Your business has an account to download the National Do Not Call Registry for every area code you call, and the numbers you call are checked against it, unless you hold the person's written consent.

## Why it matters

Sellers may not call numbers on the registry unless an exception applies: the person's written consent, or an established business relationship. The registry can be accessed only through the secure FTC site for telemarketers. The FTC says sellers, telemarketers and service providers may access it, that data for up to five area codes is free, and that fees apply from the sixth.

## How to fix

1. List the area codes of the numbers you call.
2. Create or find your account at the FTC's telemarketer registry site (telemarketing.donotcall.gov) and subscribe to those area codes. Fees apply beyond five area codes; check the current fee schedule on the FTC site.
3. Make your AI dialer, or your vendor, scrub every list against the registry before calls.
