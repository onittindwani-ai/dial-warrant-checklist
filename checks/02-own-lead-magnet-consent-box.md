# Check 02: Collect numbers with your own lead magnet and a consent box

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

This is one of the 3 checks spoken in the Reel.

## What to verify

When you collect numbers yourself (a free guide, a checklist, a booking form), the form has a box the person ticks themselves, next to wording that says you may call or text them using an automated system or an AI voice, and the form keeps the number they typed. The box is not already ticked.

## Why it matters

A number you collected yourself, with a clear yes next to it, is the strongest proof you can have. The federal definition of prior express written consent is a signed written agreement, and an electronic signature counts when it is valid under the law. Florida's law says ticking a box counts as a signature under that law. A pre-ticked box is the kit's own caution: it is hard to argue the person chose it.

## How to fix

1. Add a consent box to every form that collects a phone number. Leave it unticked.
2. Put the wording from check 04 right next to the box, in readable type.
3. Do not make the box required to download the free item, and do not tie it to a purchase (see check 05).
4. Save a copy of the form page and its wording with the date, so you can show what the person saw.
