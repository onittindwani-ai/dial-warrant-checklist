# Check 18: Scrub your list against the registry at least every 31 days

Group C: Do not call lists and stop requests

## What to verify

You download a fresh registry version no more than 31 days before any call and you keep a record of the date.

## Why it matters

Both the FTC and the FCC rules give a safe harbor for honest mistakes only if you used a version of the registry obtained no more than 31 days before the call, plus written procedures, training and your own do not call list. The FTC rule also asks for a record of when and how you accessed the registry.

## How to fix

1. Put a repeating reminder every 3 weeks to download and apply the registry.
2. Write the download date in your campaign file before launch.
3. Never launch a campaign using a list scrubbed more than 31 days ago.
