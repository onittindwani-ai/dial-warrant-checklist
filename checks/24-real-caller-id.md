# Check 24: Show a real caller ID that answers, and never fake it

Group D: Calling hours, caller ID and your phone provider

## What to verify

Calls show a number your business owns, the number connects to a person or a working line, and caller ID is never blocked or changed to look like someone else.

## Why it matters

The FCC rule requires telemarketers to transmit caller identification: the calling number and, when the carrier makes it available, the telemarketer's name. The number must let people make a do not call request during regular business hours. A telemarketer may show the seller's name and customer service number. Telemarketers may not block caller ID. The Truth in Caller ID rules make it unlawful to cause misleading or inaccurate caller ID with intent to defraud, cause harm or wrongfully obtain something of value. Florida adds a ban on deliberately displaying a different number to conceal the caller's identity.

## How to fix

1. Only call from numbers your business owns and controls.
2. Call back each outbound number yourself and see who answers.
3. Do not use 'neighbor' numbers that match the person's area code. Ask your vendor if it uses any rotating or borrowed numbers, and turn that off.
