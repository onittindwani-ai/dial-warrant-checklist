# Check 07: Keep your records for five years

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

## What to verify

Consent records, call records, scripts, do not call requests and registry downloads are kept for five years, and you know where they are.

## Why it matters

The FTC rule says records are kept for five years from the date they are produced, unless the paragraph says otherwise (some are kept five years after the item stops being used, or after a contract ends). The FCC rule also says do not call requests are kept and honored for five years. Note: an FTC guide page for a different group of callers still said 24 months on the day this kit was written. The rule text itself says five years, so follow the rule text.

## How to fix

1. Write one line in your policy: 'We keep consent, call and stop records for five years.'
2. Turn off any automatic deletion in your tools that would delete these records sooner.
3. Save the AI platform's call logs and scripts, not only your CRM.
4. Put a yearly reminder to archive, not delete.
