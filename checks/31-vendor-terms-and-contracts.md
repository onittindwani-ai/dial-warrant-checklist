# Check 31: Read your vendors' terms and keep their contracts

Group E: State laws, recording and vendors

## What to verify

You have read the terms of every vendor in your calling chain (the AI voice platform, the phone carrier, the texting service), you know who is responsible for consent and opt outs, and you keep each contract with its date.

## Why it matters

Vendor rules sit on top of the law. Twilio's messaging policy, one example, says the sender must obtain prior express consent, that consent is specific to each sender and subject, that you may not sell, rent or transfer consent, that you must keep proof and provide it on request, and that an opt out must be a single step. Its acceptable use policy makes the customer responsible for its end users' compliance and lets it suspend use. Yours may differ. The FTC rule also asks sellers to keep, for each service provider that delivers outbound calls, the contract, the date signed and the period in force.

## How to fix

1. List every vendor and find the page with its acceptable use or messaging policy.
2. Write down, for each, three answers: who gets consent, who handles opt outs, and what proof they can ask for.
3. File each signed contract with its date and its end date (templates are not needed, a folder is enough).
