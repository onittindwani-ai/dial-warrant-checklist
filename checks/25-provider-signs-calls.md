# Check 25: Ask your phone provider whether it signs your calls (STIR/SHAKEN)

Group D: Calling hours, caller ID and your phone provider

## What to verify

You have asked your phone or AI calling provider, in writing, whether it signs your outbound calls with STIR/SHAKEN (a digital signature that proves the call's caller ID was checked) and what level it assigns your numbers. You keep the answer.

## Why it matters

The FCC requires voice service providers to implement STIR/SHAKEN and to authenticate caller ID information on calls they originate. A business cannot do this itself. It can ask whether its numbers are signed at the highest level, and whether the provider uses a third party to sign them.

## How to fix

1. Email your provider: 'Do you sign our outbound calls under STIR/SHAKEN, at what attestation level, and are our numbers registered to us?' Save the reply.
2. If the answer is unclear, switch to a provider that answers clearly.
3. Re ask once a year, and every time you add new numbers.
