# Check 32: Work out your worst case before you launch

Group F: Money and upkeep

## What to verify

Before each campaign you have written the maximum exposure in a simple sum: number of calls or texts times the possible amounts per violation, and you have decided how many you will run first as a small test.

## Why it matters

The law lets a person sue for actual monetary loss or $500 per violation, whichever is greater, and a court may increase it up to three times for willful or knowing violations. The do not call rule allows up to $500 per violation to a person who got more than one call in 12 months, with the same increase. State laws add their own amounts (Florida and Oklahoma: actual damages or $500, whichever is greater, with the same increase). EXAMPLE: 500 calls x $500 = $250,000 and 500 x $1,500 = $750,000. The $750,000 figure is a ceiling that needs every call to be a willful or knowing violation and a court to choose the maximum. It is not a prediction. It shows why one bad list is costly. The kit did not verify other penalties, such as FCC fines.

## How to fix

1. Multiply: calls (or texts) x $500, then x $1,500. Write both numbers in your campaign file.
2. Start with a small batch (for example 50 numbers, all with proof) and review before scaling.
3. Set a pause rule: if you get one complaint, pause the campaign and run checks 01 to 06 on the list.
