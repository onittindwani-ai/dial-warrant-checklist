# Check 01: Name the source of every number

Group A: Your list and your proof of consent

This is one of the 3 checks spoken in the Reel.

## What to verify

Every phone number you plan to call or text has a written source: which form, event, customer record or list provider it came from, and on what date. If you cannot name the source of a number, you do not call it.

## Why it matters

The law ties permission to the person you call. For a marketing call that uses an artificial or AI voice, the rule asks for prior express written consent (a signed yes, in writing, from that person). If you cannot show where a number came from, you cannot show that person said yes. The FTC rule also expects a record of consent for each person: name, number, a copy of the request, the date.

## How to fix

1. Open templates/lead-source-register.csv (or a spreadsheet with the same columns) and list every place your phone numbers come from.
2. For each source write how people said yes (form, event, purchase, bought list) and the date range.
3. Move every number with no known source to a separate list marked DO NOT CALL until a source is found.
4. If a source cannot be proven, delete those numbers from the calling list.
