# Check 13: Tell people it is AI in the first sentence

Group B: The AI voice and the first seconds of the call

## What to verify

Your AI agent says in its first sentence that it is an automated assistant, with your business name.

## Why it matters

This is best practice, not a confirmed federal rule. In 2024 the FCC proposed rules that would require callers to tell people when a call is AI-generated. On the date this kit was written, the kit could not confirm that the proposal had become a final rule, so check its status (check 33). The FCC's 2024 ruling also said consumers can choose whether to receive AI calls, which is hard to do if they do not know the call is AI. Telling people also lowers complaints.

## How to fix

1. Start every call with a sentence like: 'Hi, this is an automated assistant calling for [Business Name].' (sample wording).
2. Do the same in the first text of a text thread when an AI writes it.
3. Check 33 each campaign: has a federal AI disclosure rule been adopted?
